📡 Desktop DX Cluster Client

Your download should be starting…

When you run the client it connects to this DX Cluster and listens on port 7300 on your local machine.

You can then connect to localhost:7300 with telnet or your favourite logging software to receive the live spot stream. Other machines on your local network can also connect to it, using the host machine's IP address on port 7300, if your firewall allows.

The client can also connect to any other UberSDR instance that has the DX Cluster add-on enabled.
